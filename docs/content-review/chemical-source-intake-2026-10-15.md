# Chemical source intake, 15 October 2026

The user wants chemical advice inside the app, without requiring each farmer to arrange an expert consultation. Current implementation already supports direct label instructions once a qualifying record exists. No separate agricultural officer approval is required by specification 0004 for that label based path. The source verification belongs to the project, not every farmer.

## Research outcome

No current exact PNG registration and matching coffee use product label was verified in this search. This is an evidence gap, not proof that suitable products are unavailable or unregistered. No live product or dose was added. The public app is unchanged.

| Source checked | What it establishes | What remains missing |
|---|---|---|
| Coffee Industry Corporation, PNG Coffee Handbook, second edition, June 2016, section 8.1 and tables 8.2 and 8.3 | Historical PNG coffee management information and named copper and systemic products | Current exact registration, current product label and complete current use restrictions |
| Chemica, Agri Chemicals supplier page | Supplier advertises fungicides for PNG, including EKO 720 | Exact coffee and target use, current local registration and complete label. Linked fungicide category returned HTTP 429, so its contents were not verified |
| NORDOX manufacturer coffee page | Manufacturer describes coffee disease uses and explicitly directs readers to national distributors for updated labels | Current PNG product, formulation, registration and local label |
| CEPA public homepage | Returned a loading page without readable registry content | Current searchable registration evidence |

The search also surfaced Philippine registration material and labels from other countries. Those do not establish PNG use. No foreign rates were imported into the PNG catalogue. Historical handbook entries and supplier marketing are research leads, not verified recommendations.

## Input needed to activate one product

A current PNG registration record and the corresponding complete label for the exact product and formulation, including coffee and target use, rate and units, application restrictions, harvest and reentry intervals, protective equipment and environmental precautions. Verify those facts and record their source, version and recheck deadline before publishing. An unknown requirement cannot be recorded as Not applicable. A label photograph alone does not establish current local registration.

With those records, the existing app can present the applicable label instructions directly, ask the required product and use questions, and include the full instructions in the PDF. It must still distinguish a possible cause from a confirmed diagnosis. Do not substitute model generated facts for missing label information.
