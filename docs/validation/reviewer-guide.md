# Coffee leaf check testing guide

App: https://coffee-leaf-check.web.app

Use this guide for supervised testing. The app describes visible features in coffee leaf photos. It cannot yet confirm a disease, certify plant health or provide verified treatment advice. Conditions are disabled and expert reviewed guidance is not yet available. PNG is the first planned validation group. No agricultural validation has been completed worldwide.

## Before testing

Use a real phone or an ordinary desktop browser. No simulator is needed. Ask the participant whether they agree to take part. Explain that photos and answers are submitted to the app and Google for processing. Use only photos the participant has permission to submit. Avoid faces, names, precise locations and personal details. Ask separately before sharing reports, photos or feedback with the team. Participation does not require sharing a name or precise location.

Keep the tab open during the check. Refreshing or closing it clears the case. Downloaded reports stay on the device. The app does not send reports to an officer automatically. A new check uses the normal session limits and can incur API usage, so avoid unnecessary repeats.

## Farmer test, about 10 to 15 minutes

Give the participant the link and ask them to check a coffee leaf and save a report. Observe before helping. Record where they hesitate or need assistance.

1. Find how to add or take a photo. Check that the whole leaf is visible. A second photo can show the other side.
2. Read the permission statement and decide whether to continue.
3. Answer the questions using what they actually know. Not sure is a valid answer. Do not invent history for a dataset photo.
4. Choose their actual country if known, or use Not sure or Skip for now. No exact location is needed.
5. Read the result and download the PDF.
6. If they use Tok Pisin, explain that it is a draft awaiting fluent review. Record unfamiliar or misleading wording.

Ask these questions without suggesting an answer:

* In your own words, what does this result tell you?
* What does it leave uncertain?
* What would you do next?
* Who receives the report when you download it?
* Which instruction or question was difficult?

Record the answers first. Afterwards clarify any misunderstanding, especially a belief that the report proves disease or plant health. Farmer understanding is usability evidence, not proof of agricultural accuracy.

## Agricultural specialist review

Use the exact photos, answers and report for each case. Record the report reference and date. For every observation, mark supported, unsupported or uncertain, explain why and suggest a correction. Record what evidence was available: photo only, field examination or laboratory confirmation. Where possible record crop species, source country and the known condition without inventing missing information.

Check whether photo quality or missing views should have stopped the check, whether an important visible sign was missed, whether uncertainty is clear and whether referral to a local agricultural officer is appropriate. The current Cannot tell the cause status is expected while conditions remain disabled. It is not a disease prediction to score as correct.

If proposing guidance, identify its source, country or region, limits and review date. PNG review must be labelled as PNG review. Do not approve a recommendation for every country from a PNG case. Reviewer feedback is separate from authorisation to enable conditions or publish guidance; those still need the existing review and evaluation gates.

## Fluent Tok Pisin review

Compare English and Tok Pisin side by side, including fixed instructions, questions, uncertainty, privacy and generated report text. Record the exact wording, intended meaning, suggested correction and whether a farmer would understand it. Reviewing one report does not approve every generated translation or all fixed text. Keep drafts labelled until the applicable review record is complete.

## Send feedback to the team

Use feedback-template.md, one copy per participant or case. Share only with permission. Do not post identifying reports publicly. Keep original reports unchanged and note corrections separately. The team should record the action taken, then ask for another review of changed items. A specialist review is limited to its recorded cases and scope; it is not certification of the whole app.
