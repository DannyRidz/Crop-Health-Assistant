- Use the deployed app at https://coffee-leaf-check.web.app, in a normal browser window. Close unrelated private tabs and hide notifications. Keep labels readable at the final video size.
- Use your existing screen recorder or an actual phone recording. A face camera is optional. Make a short audio sample first and listen for clear speech.
- Use one permitted BRACOL whole leaf image from the existing source collection. Check the [provenance and licence record](../../data-sources/bracol.md). Do not infer a disease label from its filename.
- Choose Brazil for the dataset example and Not sure for history that was not supplied. If demonstrating another country, label it as an interface example, not the photo's collection location.
- Check the app responds and screening has usable funded access. A successful health response alone does not prove Gemini availability. Keep within the existing allowance; do not buy credits or enable automatic reload.
- Keep the [script](script.md) beside the recording window. Rehearse once without dispatching repeated AI requests. The test count is dated 16 October, not an accuracy score.
- Use the deck's existing architecture diagram. It shows Firebase Hosting, Cloud Run, Gemini and Secret Manager. Explain Firestore is unnecessary because cases are temporary and PDFs stay on the device. Agent Platform and ADK are not used and their requirement remains unresolved.

## What to say when something differs

- Read the actual result before narrating. A different possible cause is not a reason to manufacture the expected text. Avoid repeated requests simply to get a preferred diagnosis.
- If the photo cannot be assessed, show the limitation or select another permitted image transparently. Do not claim a successful check when no result exists.
- If the service fails, pause the recording and investigate. A previously saved genuine result may be shown only with a clear “Previously recorded result” label. It does not prove current API availability.
- When shortening loading footage, show “Processing wait shortened.” Do not advertise the edited time as measured response speed.
- Do not describe a screen recording as a tested mobile camera journey. The physical phone camera path remains a separate verification task.
