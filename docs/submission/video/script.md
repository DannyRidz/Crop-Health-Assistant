# J2D demo narration

Working recording script, prepared 16 October 2026. No video has been recorded or uploaded. Read the quoted passages aloud; directions and headings are not narration. Either team member can narrate.

The core is about three minutes at a comfortable pace, including brief pauses. Timing is a rehearsal target, not measured recording evidence or confirmed compliance. The organiser reply about conflicting duration rules is pending. Keep the core and extension as separate editable clips. Record genuine app behaviour, not a fixed outcome staged as a new AI response.

## 1. Start with the product, about 20 seconds

Show https://coffee-leaf-check.web.app with the four steps visible. Use the app rather than a long title animation.

> A coffee farmer notices spots on a leaf. What do they mean, and what should the farmer check next? We are team J2D. Our Crop Health Assistant helps coffee farmers understand visible leaf problems through a simple photo check. It is publicly accessible worldwide, with PNG users as our first planned validation group.

## 2. Add the photo and answers, about 35 seconds

Upload one permitted BRACOL whole leaf photo. Show the permission checkbox and choose Check photos. Answer the four questions. For dataset history that is unavailable, choose Not sure. Select Brazil because that is the image source country.

> The farmer adds a coffee leaf photo and gives permission to send it for analysis. On supported phones, they can also use the camera. Four short questions add context, with a Not sure option for every question. This demonstration uses a licensed BRACOL image from Brazil. We select Brazil and leave unknown growing history as Not sure. Country is optional, and the app does not need an exact location.

During the actual request, show a short section of the existing architecture slide. Keep the original request recording for continuity. If editing out a wait, add “Processing wait shortened.”

## 3. Explain the result, about 40 seconds

Show the actual returned findings, one possible cause, its supporting evidence and next check. Read the output before narration. Do not promise that a particular disease will appear.

> The result separates what is visible from what might be causing it. Each possible cause explains why it may fit, what remains uncertain and what to check next. This makes the result useful without presenting a photograph as a confirmed diagnosis. Care steps link to published sources and match the chosen country. Coverage currently includes PNG, Indonesia, Kenya and Brazil. If the country is unknown or suitable guidance is missing, the app explains that limit instead of inventing local advice.

If no possible cause is returned, show the real outcome and say: “For this photo, the app could describe visible signs but could not support a possible cause.” Replace the first two sentences accordingly. Never overlay an invented result.

## 4. Show language and report, about 30 seconds

Switch to Tok Pisin, briefly show the same case, then prepare and download the PDF. Show its summary, photo findings and one care/source section. Keep the same case reference and country.

> Farmers can read in English or Tok Pisin. Fixed Tok Pisin wording and sample AI reports have completed language review; new case text is still automatically translated. The downloadable PDF brings the photos, findings, possible causes, care steps, sources and limitations together. The farmer can keep it or share it for follow up.

## 5. Explain the build and evidence, about 35 seconds

Show the architecture slide, then return to the app. No cloud console or credential screen is needed.

> Firebase Hosting provides the public address. Cloud Run runs the Next.js server, which calls Gemini through the official Google SDK. Secret Manager holds server credentials. Case data is processed temporarily, without a permanent farmer database. We have verified real Gemini journeys and both PDF languages, with 127 software tests passing. Independent agricultural evaluation and farmer testing are still pending. Our next step is PNG validation, followed by improvements based on the findings.

## 6. Close, about 10 seconds

Show the app address and team credits. Let credits remain readable while speaking.

> Crop Health Assistant gives coffee farmers a clearer starting point: visible evidence, possible causes and practical next checks. Thank you.

## Optional extension, about 35 to 45 seconds

Use only if the confirmed duration permits it. Insert after the result section. Show the actual country editor and a real change to Not sure. Wait for the update before export. If Brazil is restored, explain that it is the image source country.

> Changing the country updates the available care without changing the photo findings. Choosing Not sure keeps the observations but withholds care that depends on a known country. The app also contains chemical label checks, but currently offers no chemical products or spray doses because verified local registrations and matching labels are not yet available. Those details cannot safely be guessed from a leaf photo. This distinction keeps the useful screening result separate from treatment claims that still need evidence.
